## ✨ What is Mestri?

Finding the right person for a job shouldn't mean asking five people, searching through WhatsApp groups, or calling random numbers.

**Mestri brings it all into one place.**

👷 **Operators** → showcase their skills & find nearby work
🏠 **Work Providers** → find the right person for their job
📍 **Location-based discovery** → connect locally
⭐ **Profiles & ratings** → make better decisions
🤝 **Direct connection** → talk directly and get the work done

## ⚡ Core Features

### 👷 Find Skilled Operators

Browse workers based on their **skills, category, location, ratings and services**.

### 📋 Post a Work

Need something done?

Create a work request with the important details and make it visible to suitable operators.

### 🎯 Smart Matching

Mestri helps bring together **the right work with the right people nearby**.

### ⭐ Build Trust

Explore operator profiles, skills, experience and ratings before making a decision.

### 📍 Local Discovery

Find opportunities and services **around your area**, instead of searching through scattered platforms.

### 🤝 Direct Connection

Once you've found the right person, connect directly and discuss the work.

## 💡 Why Mestri?

Traditional local hiring often looks like:

```text
Ask someone
    ↓
Call someone
    ↓
"Do you know anyone?"
    ↓
More calls
    ↓
Maybe find someone 😵‍💫
```

Mestri turns that into:

```text
Post / Search
      ↓
Discover
      ↓
Compare
      ↓
Connect
      ↓
Done. ✅
```

**Less searching. More doing.**

## 🌱 The Bigger Idea

Mestri is built around a simple idea:

> **Skills deserve visibility.**

A carpenter, electrician, mechanic, driver, farmer, painter or repair technician shouldn't need a large company behind them to find opportunities.

Mestri gives local skilled workers a **digital presence** and makes it easier for people to find the right person when they need work done.
